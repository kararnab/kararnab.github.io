ConnectionsYour contacts. Your phone. Nobody else’s.

Connections is a contacts app for Android that keeps your address book in an encrypted database on your device. It has no Internet permission, so it cannot send your contacts anywhere.

Get it onGoogle Play

Available for Android. Read the Connections privacy policy

Who it’s for

Android users who want an address book that stays private and out of reach of other apps.

What it helps you do

Keep the people who matter in one place, and reach them through the app you choose.

Where it stands

Available on Google Play for Android.

Your next step

Install Connections and import your contacts from a vCard file.

What it helps you do

A private address book, built to stay that way.

Encrypted on this device

Contact details and photos live in a database encrypted with a key protected by your phone’s Android Keystore.

No Internet access

The app doesn’t have the Internet permission, and it isn’t included in Android cloud backup.

Paper backup

Print your contacts as QR codes, optionally protected with a password, and scan them back in later.

Import from vCard

Bring contacts in from a vCard file you select — nothing is read from your phone’s address book.

Reach people your way

Call, message, WhatsApp, email or share a contact through the app you pick.

Made for every screen

Light and dark themes, phone and tablet layouts, and translations into Hindi, Bengali, Chinese (Simplified), Spanish and Portuguese.

Inside the app

The developer can’t see your contacts

Connections doesn’t collect personal data and uses no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. Details leave the app only when you choose to share them.
