Questions

Good questions, honest answers.

When is 1Chat coming out?

It’s in final testing on Android. It comes to Google Play first, then to the App Store for iPhone. Ask for early access to try it before launch.

Can 1Chat read my messages?

No. Messages, voice notes and documents are end-to-end encrypted: they are locked on your phone and unlocked only on the recipient’s. Our servers pass them along but can’t read them, and you can check a contact’s security code to confirm who you’re talking to.

What do I need to sign up?

Just your phone number. 1Chat sends a one-time code by SMS to confirm it’s yours. If you allow it, 1Chat can show which of your contacts already use it.

Is 1Chat free?

Yes. Messages, voice and video calls, backups and moving to a new phone are all free, with no ads. We may add optional paid extras later, but chatting with your people stays free.

Does 1Chat show ads or track me?

No. There are no ads and no analytics or advertising SDKs, and we don’t sell your data. The privacy policy lists exactly what is stored and for how long.

What happens to my chats when I change phones?

Move them across directly: 1Chat transfers every chat, photo and file to your new phone over Wi-Fi, or over a direct connection when the phones share no network. Or restore an encrypted backup when you set up the new phone.

Are my backups safe?

Backups are encrypted on your phone with a password or a 64-digit key that only you know, before they reach Google Drive or the folder you choose. Not even 1Chat or Google can open them. Keep the password or key safe: without it, a backup can’t be restored.

Which languages does 1Chat speak?

English, Hindi, Bengali, Chinese, Spanish, Arabic and French, with an always-English option if you prefer.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, at any time, from the app or by email. Your account is deleted from our servers 30 days after you ask, and logging back in during those 30 days cancels it. How to delete your account.
