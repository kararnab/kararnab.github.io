AVPlayerYour own streaming service, on your own hardware.

AVPlayer is a self-hosted media project for films, series and music. Add a video and it becomes an adaptive stream with subtitles and seek previews, ready for your phone, computer, browser or TV.

Status reviewed November 2026 · In development; no release date set.

Ask about early access
Release date not setGoogle PlayPrivate for nowGitHub

AVPlayer is in development and hasn’t been released. Its source code lives in a private GitHub repository for now.

Who it’s for

People who keep their own collection of films, series and music, and have a computer or home server to run it on.

What it helps you do

Stream your personal library to every screen you use — without handing it to a cloud service.

Where it stands

In development. Not yet available to download.

Your next step

Ask about early access and testing the self-hosted server.

What’s being built

A full streaming experience for a library you own.

Films and series

Home rails, seasons and episodes, search, My List, Continue Watching and next-episode autoplay.

A music player too

Albums and tags read on upload, playlists, a play queue with shuffle and repeat, and a mini player.

Built for the couch

A TV mode you can drive entirely with a remote, plus phone, desktop and web clients.

Watch offline

Download titles for the trip on Android, iOS and desktop.

Profiles for everyone

Up to five profiles per account, with age limits, a Kids mode and an optional PIN.

Yours to run

A lean Go server that starts with Docker Compose, with sign-in, roles and an admin console.
