Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app 1Chat (package name com.oneloom.onechat), a messaging and calling app, and to the servers that operate it. 1Chat is in development and is not yet available on Google Play.

1Chat is developed and published by ARNAB KAR, an individual developer based in India, under the Google Play developer name A Storyteller. ARNAB KAR is responsible for the app and for this Privacy Policy. In this policy, "we", "us" and "the developer" refer to ARNAB KAR. This policy is hosted on the developer's website, 1loom.com. Your use of 1Chat is also governed by the 1Chat Terms of Service.

Summary

  • You sign in with your phone number. We use it to verify you and to let people who have your number find you.
  • Messages, voice notes, documents and calls pass through our servers to reach the people you talk to.
  • Messages, voice notes and documents are end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted on your phone and can be decrypted only on the recipient's phone; our servers cannot read them. Calls are encrypted in transit but not end-to-end.
  • Undelivered messages and shared voice notes and documents are deleted 30 days after they are sent.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • You can delete your account in the app, or by email if you no longer have it (how to delete your account). Your account is removed from our servers 30 days later; until then, logging in again restores it. See Deleting your account.

Information We Collect

Account information

Your phone number is required to create an account. To verify it, we send a one-time code by SMS; the code is stored on our servers for up to 5 minutes and can be used only once. We also keep records of your signed-in devices (sessions) so that you stay signed in and can be signed out.

Profile information

Your name, and optionally a profile photo and an "About" text, are stored on our servers and are visible to other 1Chat users who can contact you. We also store your profile settings, such as who can see when you were last online and whether you send read receipts.

Contacts

If you allow access to your contacts, 1Chat sends the phone numbers in your address book, together with the names you have saved for them, to our servers to find out which of your contacts use 1Chat. These contact details are used only for that check and are not stored on our servers afterwards. Contacts who use 1Chat are saved in the app on your device. You can use 1Chat without giving contacts access, but you will not see which of your contacts use it.

Messages and media

When you send a message, voice note, document, contact card or location, it is sent to our servers and forwarded to the recipient. A message waiting for a recipient who is offline is held on our servers until it is delivered, and then removed; an undelivered message is deleted 30 days after it was sent. Voice notes and documents are stored on our servers so the recipient can download them, and are deleted 30 days after they were sent. Profile photos are kept until you replace them or your account is deleted.

Messages, voice notes and documents between people are end-to-end encrypted with the Signal protocol: they are encrypted on your phone, and only the recipient's phone holds the keys to decrypt them. Our servers store and forward them without being able to read them. Our servers do see the information needed to deliver them: who sent a message to whom and when, and the size of voice notes and documents. Messages exchanged with automated services (bots) are not end-to-end encrypted.

To set up encryption, your phone uploads its public encryption keys to our servers, where other users' phones can fetch them. Your private keys never leave your phone. Each chat has a security code that you and your contact can compare to check that your messages are encrypted to the right phone.

Calls

Voice and video calls are routed through our call servers so that they work across different networks. Call audio and video are encrypted in transit, but not end-to-end, and calls are not recorded. Your call history is stored only on your device.

Location

1Chat accesses your location only when you choose to share it in a chat. The location you share is sent as a message to that chat. The app does not track your location in the background.

Usage and technical information

To provide messaging features, our servers process your online status and last-seen time, typing indicators, and delivery and read receipts, and store the list of users you have blocked. Our servers also keep technical logs, which include IP addresses, app and device information, and the time of requests, to keep the service secure and to fix problems.

Diagnostics

The app does not send analytics or crash reports. Any diagnostic events the app records stay on your device.

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions. Optional permissions can be refused or revoked in Android settings.

  • Internet and network state: to send and receive messages and calls.
  • Contacts (optional): to find which of your contacts use 1Chat, as described above.
  • Microphone: for voice and video calls and voice notes. Required to make or answer a call.
  • Camera (optional): for video calls, photos and scanning QR codes. If you refuse camera access, calls continue with audio only.
  • Location (optional): only when you choose to share your location in a chat. On some Android versions it is also needed to set up a direct connection when you transfer chats to a new phone; 1Chat does not use it to find your location then.
  • Local network, nearby Wi-Fi devices and Wi-Fi state (optional): only to transfer your chats directly to your new phone.
  • Notifications and full-screen notifications: to alert you to messages and to show incoming calls.
  • Bluetooth: to route call audio to Bluetooth headsets.
  • Audio settings: to switch call audio between the earpiece, speaker and headsets.
  • Foreground service, wake lock and start at boot: added automatically by Android libraries used for calls and background work, to keep calls and scheduled tasks running reliably. They are not used to collect personal data.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • create and secure your account and verify your phone number;
  • send you service messages by SMS, such as a warning before an unused account is deleted;
  • deliver your messages, media and calls, and show delivery status;
  • help you find contacts who use 1Chat;
  • enforce blocks and protect the service against abuse; and
  • diagnose and fix problems with the service.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

How Information Is Shared

  • With the people you communicate with: the messages, media and calls you send, and your profile information, according to your privacy settings.
  • With service providers that host or operate parts of the service on our behalf, such as cloud hosting for our servers and an SMS provider that delivers verification codes and service messages (it receives your phone number and the message). They may use this information only to provide these services to us.
  • When required by law, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of our users, the public or the service.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your chat history, call history and the list of your contacts who use 1Chat are stored in the app on your device. Sign-in credentials are encrypted with a key protected by your device's Android Keystore. The app's data is excluded from Android cloud backup, so it is not copied to your Google account backup. Deleting your account in the app also deletes this data from that device.

Chat backups

Backups are off until you turn them on. A backup holds your messages, chat list, call history, contacts who use 1Chat and blocked accounts, and, if you choose, your photos, voice notes and documents. It is encrypted on your device with a password or a 64-digit key that you set. We never receive your password or key, and without it nobody can read the backup, including us and Google. Automatic backups run daily, weekly or monthly only if you choose a schedule.

You choose where a backup is kept: in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, or in a folder you pick on your device or another storage provider. For Google Drive, 1Chat asks for access only to its own hidden app folder (the drive.appdata permission), not to your other files. The backup uses your Drive storage, and Google's terms and privacy policy apply to it. Backups go straight from your phone to the place you chose and never pass through our servers. Restoring is offered when you set up 1Chat on a phone. To delete a Drive backup, remove 1Chat's hidden app data in Google Drive (Settings › Manage apps); to delete a backup file, delete the file.

Moving to a new phone

You can also transfer your chats straight to a new phone over your Wi-Fi network, or over a temporary direct Wi-Fi connection between the two phones. The transfer is encrypted with a one-time code that the new phone shows for you to scan, and it does not pass through our servers.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Verification codes: up to 5 minutes, and deleted once used.
  • Undelivered messages: until delivered, or at most 30 days after they are sent.
  • Shared voice notes and documents (encrypted): 30 days after they are sent.
  • Contacts sent for matching: not stored.
  • Account, profile, profile photo, blocks and sessions: until your account is deleted, which happens 30 days after you ask to delete it (see below), or after 120 days in which 1Chat hasn't connected to our servers from your account. We send an SMS to your number 40 days before an unused account is deleted; opening 1Chat keeps it. Expired and signed-out sessions are deleted automatically.
  • Server logs: kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.
  • Chat backups: kept in your Google Drive or the folder you chose until you delete them. We do not hold a copy.

Some technical logs may include your account identifier or IP address after your account is deleted; they are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

Deleting Your Account

These steps are also on a page of their own: Delete your 1Chat account.

In the app

Open 1Chat and go to Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or your phone, email arnabrocking@gmail.com with the subject "1Chat account deletion" and the phone number of your account, in international format (for example +91 XXXXX XXXXX), the name on your 1Chat profile and roughly when you last used 1Chat. To protect your account from deletion by someone else, we then send a one-time code by SMS to that number, which you must send back to us within 7 days. We complete verified requests within 30 days, or tell you within that time if we need longer. The full steps are on the account deletion page.

What happens

  • Immediately: you are signed out on all your devices, your profile no longer appears to other 1Chat users, and nobody can send you new messages. If you delete from the app, the chat history, call history and contacts stored in the app on that device are deleted too, and cannot be recovered.
  • After 30 days: your account is permanently deleted from our servers, including your phone number record, profile, profile photo, sessions, encryption keys, blocks and list of chats.
  • Changing your mind: if you log in again with the same phone number during those 30 days, the deletion is cancelled and your account is restored. Chat history deleted from your device is not restored.
  • What is not deleted: messages you sent remain on the recipients' devices. Messages, voice notes and documents waiting on our servers are deleted 30 days after they were sent, as described above. Chat backups you made are not deleted either; you can delete them yourself, as described under Chat backups.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit, refresh tokens are stored on our servers only as one-way hashes, and sign-in credentials on your device are protected by the Android Keystore. Messages, voice notes and documents are end-to-end encrypted, as explained above. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please also protect your device with a screen lock.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can edit your name, photo and "About" text in the app at any time.
  • You can refuse or revoke contacts, camera, microphone and location access in Android settings.
  • You can block other users.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact the developer using the details below.

Age Restrictions

1Chat is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
