OpenNote Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to OpenNote: ScribblePad ("OpenNote"), including the Android app with package name com.kararnab.bestnote, available on Google Play. OpenNote is a multiplatform note-taking application available on Android, iOS, Desktop, and Web.

OpenNote is developed and published by ARNAB KAR, an individual developer based in India, under the Google Play developer name A Storyteller. ARNAB KAR is responsible for the app and for this Privacy Policy. In this policy, "we" and "us" refer to ARNAB KAR. This policy is hosted on the developer's website, 1loom.com.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use OpenNote.

Data Collection

OpenNote currently operates without internet access and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

All notes and content are stored locally on your device. The developer has no access to your notes.

On Android, the app's data (your notes and app settings) may be included in Android's standard device backup to your Google account, if you have turned on backup in your device settings. This backup is provided and controlled by Google and your device, not by the developer, and the developer cannot access it.

If you choose to enable optional features in the future (such as cloud backup, synchronization, or account-based services), we will collect only the data necessary to provide those features, such as your email address and note content.

How Your Data Is Used

Currently, your data is used only locally within the app.

When you choose to share or export a note (for example as a PDF), the file is created on your device and passed to the app you select. That app's own privacy policy then applies. Links in the app (such as to the developer's website or the Google Play page) open in your browser or the Play Store.

If optional features such as sync or backup are enabled, your data will be used only for:

  • Syncing notes across your devices
  • Providing backup and restore functionality
  • Maintaining app performance and reliability

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Ads & Monetization

OpenNote currently contains no advertisements and no advertising SDKs. In the future, the free version of OpenNote may include advertisements. If ads are added, this policy will be updated to name the advertising providers before that version is released.

Advertising providers may collect limited information such as device identifiers, IP address, and app usage data to display relevant ads, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

The premium version of OpenNote will remain ad-free.

Data Storage & Security

Currently, all data is stored locally on your device.

If cloud features are introduced, we will implement security measures such as encryption in transit (HTTPS) and secure storage practices.

Permissions

The Android app currently does not request any special permissions, including Internet access.

Future features may require:

  • Internet access (for syncing and backups)
  • Storage access (for saving and loading notes)
  • Media access (for attachments)

Permissions will only be used for core functionality.

Third-Party Services

OpenNote currently does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

Future features may rely on trusted third-party services for syncing, backups, or ads. These services will be used only to support app functionality.

Children’s Privacy

OpenNote is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If we become aware that personal data from a child has been collected without parental consent, we will delete it.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your notes stay on your device until you delete them in the app (notes moved to the bin can be deleted permanently), clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Because the developer does not receive your notes, there is nothing for the developer to retain or delete on your behalf.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, update, or delete your data.

If account-based features are introduced, you will be able to request deletion of your data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of OpenNote after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
