Delete in the App

This page explains how to delete your account in 1Chat, the messaging app by ARNAB KAR (Google Play developer: A Storyteller).

  1. Open 1Chat on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out right away.

Delete Without the App

If you still have 1Chat, deleting in the app is faster. If you no longer have the app or your phone, you can ask us by email. Because deletion cannot be undone once the 30 days pass, we first make sure the request really comes from the person who holds the number:

  1. Email arnabrocking@gmail.com with the subject "1Chat account deletion". Include the phone number of your account in international format (for example +91 XXXXX XXXXX), the name shown on your 1Chat profile, and roughly when you last used 1Chat.
  2. We send a one-time code by SMS to that number. Reply to our email with the code within 7 days. If we don't receive the correct code in time, the request is closed and you can start again.
  3. Once the number is confirmed, we schedule the deletion and email you. The same 30-day period as in the app then applies (see What Is Deleted).

We complete verified requests within 30 days, or tell you within that time if we need longer. We will never ask for your password, an identity document or payment to delete your account.

If you no longer have the number: you don't need to do anything. Accounts that are not used for 120 days are deleted automatically (see below).

Accounts Not Used for 120 Days

If 1Chat hasn't connected to our servers from your account for 120 days, your account is permanently deleted from our servers, as described below. Forty days before that, we send an SMS to your number to warn you. Opening 1Chat on your phone at any time before then keeps your account.

What Is Deleted

Immediately:

  • you are signed out on your phone, and your profile no longer appears to other 1Chat users;
  • nobody can send you new messages;
  • if you delete from the app, the chat history, call history and contacts stored in the app on that phone are deleted too, and cannot be recovered.

After 30 days, your account is permanently deleted from our servers, including:

  • your phone number record and sessions;
  • your name, profile photo and "About" text;
  • your encryption keys (public keys only: private keys never leave your phone);
  • your list of blocked users and your list of chats.

Changing your mind: if you sign in again with the same phone number during those 30 days, the deletion is cancelled and your account is restored. Chat history deleted from your phone is not restored.

What Is Kept, and for How Long

  • Messages you sent stay on the recipients' phones; we cannot delete them.
  • Encrypted messages, voice notes and documents still waiting on our servers are deleted 30 days after they were sent.
  • Server logs, which may include your account identifier or IP address, are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

See the 1Chat Privacy Policy for details.
